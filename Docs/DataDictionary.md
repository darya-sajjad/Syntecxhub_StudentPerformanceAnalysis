# Data Dictionary

# About Dataset
This is a student performance dataset, it contains:

- Columns: 16
- Records: 25,000 (Before Cleaning) & 15,000 (After Cleaning)

# Columns Overview

## student_id
A unique identification number for every student. Used as the primary key to track students and prevent duplicate records.

## age
Tracks the age of a student

## gender
Tracks the gender of a student

## school_type
Tracks the type of school a student studies in (e.g. public, private)

## parent_education
Tracks the level of education on the student's parent

## study_hours
Tracks the number of hours a student studies on average

## attendance_percentage
Tracks the attendance percentage of a student

## internet_access
Tracks is a student has internet access or not

## travel_time
Tracks the time it takes a student to reach the school

## extra_activities
Tracks if a student participates in any extra activities or not

## study_method
Tracks the type of study method a student follows (e.g. uses notes, studies in group, gets coaching, etc)

## math_score
Tracks the score of a student in maths subject

## science_score
Tracks the score of a student in science subject

## english_score
Tracks the score of a student in english subject

## overall_score
Tracks the overall score of a student

## final_grade
Tracks the grade a student recieves according to their overall_score
